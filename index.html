How Atlas Grounds Data & Stays Compliant

1. Realistic Listing & Neighborhood Data (No Fictional Gov APIs)

Israeli cities and the Tabu land registry do not offer unified MLS feeds or open APIs for live listings. Atlas combines direct structured feeds from participating partner brokers/developers, real public parcel/zoning reference layers where published, and local specialist verification for schools, synagogues, commutes, and safety.

2. Undocumented Space Protection

Converted basements, enclosed balconies, and roof additions rarely appear in public records. Atlas compares seller declarations against uploaded Tabu deeds and municipal Arnona bills—and dispatches an independent licensed appraiser before an offer is signed.

3. Legal & Broker Ethics Guardrails

Your real estate lawyer leads the contract and protects your interests. Per Israel Bar Assoc. Law Sec. 58, Atlas charges ₪0 lawyer referral or split fees. Partner brokerage introductions operate through a licensed entity with explicit written consent (2025 Brokers Ethics), separate from FX, lending, and insurance partners.
